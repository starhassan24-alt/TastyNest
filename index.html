Healthy Meal Prep Guide

7 Healthy Family Dinner Recipes That Picky Eaters Will Love

⏱ Prep Time: 10 Mins 🍳 Cook Time: 20 Mins 🥗 Diet: High Protein & Clean Eating 👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Servings: Family of 4

Finding dinner ideas that are nutritious, quick to cook, and approved by kids can be an exhausting challenge. These healthy family dinner recipes focus on whole food ingredients, balanced nutrients, and simple one-pan cooking methods that cut dishwashing in half.

1. One-Pan Mediterranean Lemon Herb Chicken & Roasted Potatoes

Prep Time: 10 mins | Cook Time: 20 mins

Ingredients: 1.5 lbs boneless chicken breast, 1 lb baby gold potatoes (halved), 1 cup fresh green beans, 3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil, 4 cloves minced garlic, 1 tbsp dried oregano, fresh juice of 1 lemon.

Instructions: Place chicken cutlets and halved potatoes on a large baking tray. Drizzle with olive oil, minced garlic, oregano, salt, and lemon juice. Roast at 400°F (200°C) for 15 minutes, add green beans, and roast for another 7 minutes until chicken reaches 165°F.

2. Sheet-Pan Wild Honey Garlic Salmon & Broccoli Florets

Prep Time: 8 mins | Cook Time: 15 mins

Ingredients: 4 salmon fillets, 3 cups broccoli florets, 2 tbsp honey, 2 tbsp low-sodium soy sauce (or tamari), 1 tbsp olive oil, 1 tsp sesame seeds.

Instructions: Whisk honey, soy sauce, and minced garlic in a small bowl. Arrange salmon and broccoli on a foil-lined baking sheet. Brush glaze generously over salmon and toss broccoli in olive oil. Bake at 400°F for 12-14 minutes until salmon flakes tenderly with a fork.

3. Hidden-Veggie Turkey Bolognese Whole-Wheat Pasta

Prep Time: 10 mins | Cook Time: 15 mins

Ingredients: 1 lb lean ground turkey (93/7), 1 finely grated zucchini, 1 grated carrot, 1 jar organic marinara sauce, 12 oz whole wheat penne pasta, 1/2 cup shredded mozzarella.

Instructions: Brown the turkey in a skillet over medium heat. Stir in finely grated zucchini and carrots (they melt invisibly into the sauce so picky kids won't notice). Pour in marinara sauce and simmer for 5 minutes. Toss with cooked whole-wheat penne and top with light mozzarella.

4. 15-Minute Crispy Black Bean & Sweet Corn Tacos

Prep Time: 5 mins | Cook Time: 10 mins

Ingredients: 8 warm corn tortillas, 1 can rinsed black beans, 1 cup sweet corn, 1 tsp ground cumin, 1 sliced avocado, fresh lime wedges and cilantro.

Instructions: Sauté black beans and sweet corn in a skillet with cumin, garlic powder, and a splash of lime juice for 5 minutes. Warm corn tortillas and fill with bean mixture. Top with avocado slices and salsa for a high-fiber, plant-powered family meal.
